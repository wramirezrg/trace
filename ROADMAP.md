# Roadmap

This roadmap describes the direction of TRACE. It reflects current priorities and may change as
development progresses; no dates are guaranteed.

## Shipped

TRACE is free to use, with all current features included: no trial, no paid tier.

- **Live Monitoring**: real-time digital and analog signal graphing, multiple synchronized
  panels, engineering-unit scaling, alarms, and a conditional recording trigger
- **Replay**: historical session playback, dual time cursors, combining several recordings onto
  one timeline, and post-hoc signal processing
- **CSV Export and encrypted recording** for reporting and offline analysis
- **Chain Map**: industrial Ethernet topology and reachability diagnostics for daisy chains
- **Pinout Library**: reference for industrial connector standards (RJ45, M12, DB9, terminal
  blocks)
- **Simulation Mode**: 13 configurable digital/analog signal patterns, ready-made scenarios,
  manual events (force/freeze/spike-dip), and simulated communication interruption, all without a
  PLC

## Exploring

Priorities being evaluated for future releases, not commitments:

- Support for additional industrial protocols beyond EtherNet/IP
- Continued refinement of the simulation toolset (more patterns, more scenarios)
- General usability and workflow improvements based on field feedback
